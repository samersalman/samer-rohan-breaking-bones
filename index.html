What is this research, anyway?

  1. Surgical outcomes research

    Surgery does not end when the patient leaves the operating room. Outcomes research asks what happens next: who goes home, who needs rehab, who has a complication, who is back to normal in a year. It uses records from thousands of patients to find patterns no single surgeon could see.

  2. AI in surgery

    Computers can learn patterns from past patients and use them to estimate what is likely to happen to a new one. That can help a care team plan earlier. It does not replace a clinician's judgement, and a prediction is a probability, not a promise.

  3. Why we share it here

    Journal articles are written for other specialists. Here we say the same thing in everyday words, then keep a Technical view for readers who want the details.
